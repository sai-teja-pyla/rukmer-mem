How accurate is the AI analysis?

SnapReport AI uses Claude Sonnet 4, the most advanced vision-language model. It identifies progress, quality issues, and safety concerns with industry-leading accuracy. You always review reports before sending.

Is my data secure?

Yes. All images and reports are encrypted in transit and at rest. We never share your data with third parties. You can delete your data at any time.

Can I customize the reports?

Pro and Business plans include custom branding. You can add your logo, company colors, and customize report templates to match your style.

What image formats are supported?

We support JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WEBP. Images can be up to 10MB each. There's no limit on image resolution.
